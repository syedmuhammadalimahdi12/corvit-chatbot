Admission

Paid batch or NAVTTC (free)?

Both routes lead to the same courses — the difference is cost, timing and eligibility.

The details below are examples. Ask the assistant or your campus for the current fee, eligibility and dates.

NAVTTC (free)

  • Government-sponsored — no course fee for eligible students
  • Limited seats, fixed cohort start dates
  • Requires meeting an education/age eligibility criteria
  • Apply via the NAVTTC portal before the deadline

Paid batch

  • Self-funded, flexible morning/evening/weekend timing
  • Rolling admissions — start sooner
  • No government eligibility restrictions
  • Register directly with the campus

How to apply

Four steps to your first class

  1. Pick a course & batch type

    Use the course list above, or ask the assistant for a recommendation.

  2. Register with the campus

    Visit, call, or message the campus you'd like to attend.

  3. Submit documents

    CNIC and educational certificates — plus NAVTTC eligibility proof if applicable.

  4. Confirm your seat

    Pay the fee (paid batches) or receive NAVTTC confirmation, then attend orientation.
